# Part 2 Roadmap — Research Paper Organizer

Features planned for the second progress/final version:
1. Automatic keyword extraction from research notes.
2. Image insertion into notes.
3. APA citation generation and citation metadata management.
4. Export notes to PDF and DOCX.
5. Testing, bug fixes, UI polish, report, and final demo.

LaTeX document generation and BibTeX citation output are intentionally excluded.
